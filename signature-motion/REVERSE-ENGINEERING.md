# Reverse-engineering reference films

The six shipped Signature Motion scenes are useful compositional primitives: typography, evidence, charts, workflow alignment, layered media, and geometric titles. The seven advanced recipes below are **design proposals**, not implemented renderers or verified application integrations. They extract reusable relationships from actual films rather than reproduce their assets, scripts, or brand identities. Current pack samples contain fictional demonstration content.

Research checked on **7 November 2026**. Evidence is separated into sampled video frames, creator explanations, and our own design inferences. Frame samples establish visible states and approximate progression; they do not establish every cut, exact transition timing, or audio synchronization. No complete audiovisual viewing or sound analysis is claimed. All beat recipes are **new proposed edits**, not original-film timecodes. Reference media used for inspection remains outside this repository.

## 1. Zach King: impossible state, then reinterpretation

**Reference:** [Zach Kings Magic Broomstick](https://www.tiktok.com/@zachking/video/6768504823336815877), posted 9 December 2019. [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/689105-most-viewed-video-on-tiktok) records approximately **2.2 billion views, verified by TikTok on 15 March 2022**. This is historical reach, not a current count or a count of unique viewers.

**Evidence:** Direct TikTok playback was unavailable. The official embed endpoint recognizes the video ID, but no frame-level reconstruction is claimed. In his [filmed production explanation](https://www.vanityfair.com/video/watch/zach-king-prop-tour), Zach describes a mirror, a broomstick with attached costume legs, an electric skateboard, and coordinated camera/performer movement. This is a practical shooting illusion, not simply an editing effect.

**Mechanism:** An immediately legible impossibility gives the viewer a question to resolve. A later reveal changes the interpretation of earlier imagery. Rewatch potential is a reasonable design hypothesis; the view total does not prove which element caused it.

**Proposed recipe — Impossible / Reveal, 15s:** 0–2s show the impossible state; 2–6s sustain it while preserving the deceptive viewpoint; 6–10s move the camera or an occluder to reveal the mechanism; 10–13s deliver the resulting joke or benefit; 13–15s close cleanly.

**Agent inputs:** Staged footage or a controlled 3D scene, camera path, occluder/subject masks, necessary clean plates, reveal point, and payoff. The agent should generate a capture plan before requesting footage.

**Failure condition:** Arbitrary footage lacks the required geometry. A visible seam, inconsistent reflection, or premature viewpoint change breaks the illusion. Offer a guided shoot or a simpler transition instead. This belongs in the video editor’s planning and previz workflow as much as its editor.

## 2. Nike: match movement across a stable seam

**Reference:** [You Can’t Stop Us, Wieden+Kennedy](https://www.wk.com/work/nike-you-cant-stop-us/), July 2020; [official NIKE JAPAN upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GbQomqb28os), dated 30 July 2020. Search retrieval of that official upload showed **7,621,523 views**; this is an indexed snapshot, not a live counter. It is distinct from larger, inconsistently reported campaign totals.

**Evidence:** Sampled frames from the agency’s public 90.17s MP4. Around 0–4s, complementary athletes share a central composition; 20s pairs tennis/baseball swings; 40s aligns fallen bodies; 44–48s moves through empty and improvised sporting spaces; 52–60s introduces collective responses; 64–84s returns to action and celebration. These are sampled states, not exact cut boundaries.

**Mechanism:** The seam remains predictable while footage and meaning change. Gesture, subject scale, camera angle, and motion phase make separate events briefly read as one. Emotional progression gives the matching technique a narrative purpose.

**Proposed recipe — Matchline, 18s:** 0–3s establish one unmistakable match; 3–8s show two contrasting pairs; 8–12s introduce a shared obstacle; 12–15s resolve it through a stronger pairing; 15–18s hold the conclusion.

**Agent inputs:** Licensed clips, action timestamps, subject landmarks, crop bounds, motion direction, permissible retiming, and the editorial relationship between each pair. Rank candidates by both geometric compatibility and meaning; preview before committing the match.

**Failure condition:** Similar colors alone do not make a match. Excessive warping, incompatible camera perspective, or different action phases exposes the join. Start with translation, crop, scale, and limited retiming. This is a strong next implementation for the video editor and the planned Parallel Universe family.

## 3. Figma Slides: workplace tension earns the product reveal

**Reference:** [All Hands on Deck, IVERSON](https://iverson.inc/work/figma), a 2025 campaign; [official Figma film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZI8KRElcjV4). IVERSON reports **2M+ organic views across social**. Treat this as creator-reported reach. The [2026 Webby B2B award](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2026/video-film/branded-entertainment/b2b/373669/figma-slides-all-hands-on-deck) independently confirms craft recognition. Neither establishes conversion impact; company revenue growth cannot be attributed to this film from the available evidence.

**Evidence:** Sampled frames from IVERSON’s 69.55s hosted version show an office setup at 0.5–3s, presentation content at 7s, escalating workplace imagery, a collaborative slide interface at 24s, a crowd around the laptop at 30s, a typing-status suspense cue at 43s, and group reactions at 50–58s. Different hosted versions may have different lengths.

**Mechanism:** Viewers recognize the situation before they evaluate the software. The product appears inside a consequential task, and the reaction completes the payoff. The useful structure is pressure → useful action → anticipation → relief.

**Proposed recipe — Approval Drama, 18s:** 0–3s establish a familiar problem and deadline; 3–6s escalate one obstacle; 6–11s show a real product action; 11–14s hold the anticipated response; 14–18s deliver the reaction and closing proposition.

**Agent inputs:** Actor/creator footage or approved character assets, a concrete scenario, real interface capture, reaction shots, transcript/dialogue timings, and the promised outcome.

**Failure condition:** The product action does not explain the improvement, or the setup consumes the entire short. Preserve a visible causal connection and avoid fabricated customer reactions. the video editor can assemble this structure; a motion-only fallback can use supplied messages and interface states.

## 4. Linear Agent: direct attention through depth and focus

**Reference:** [Introducing Linear Agent](https://linear.app/changelog/2026-03-24-introducing-linear-agent), **24 March 2026**. No independently established popularity measure was found for the inspected asset. Use it as a contemporary product-film reference.

**Evidence:** Sampled frames from the official 54.9s embedded MP4 show an angled workspace at 0.5–3s, an agent input panel at 7–11s, relevant issues at 16s, a follow-up request at 20s, a specification at 25s, an architecture view at 30–35s, the broader workspace at 40s, and a restrained brand finish around 46–52s.

**Mechanism:** Camera scale, plane angle, contrast, and selective focus guide attention through connected product states. The film keeps one product world recognizable while changing what deserves attention. Darkness is a supporting choice; the essential technique is controlled visual priority.

**Proposed recipe — Obsidian Product Journey, 16s:** 0–3s establish a task in its workspace; 3–6s move toward the relevant control; 6–10s show the resulting state; 10–13s reveal the usable artifact in context; 13–16s hold a benefit and brand.

**Agent inputs:** Genuine screen recordings or state captures, focal rectangles, interaction timestamps, supported feature claims, camera poses, focus ranges, and reading holds. Store the task/result relationship alongside the visuals.

**Failure condition:** Perspective and blur make the claimed benefit impossible to inspect. Do not animate invented UI or imply that decorative waiting time measures actual execution. Use a flatter camera and larger readable result when portrait space is limited. This is directly relevant to the video editor product demos and the research agent research-result walkthroughs.

## 5. Nothing / BUCK: one visual grammar, expanding freedom

**Reference:** [Nothing, Playground](https://buck.co/work/nothing-playground). BUCK’s [2025 retrospective](https://pencilsdown.buck.co/p/the-2025-roundup) establishes the year; an exact primary-source release day was not verified. No reliable public view total was established.

**Evidence:** Timestamped frames from the actual 62.25s hero asset show geometric blocks and botanical contours at 0–6s, more conspicuous rectangular constraints at 8–14s, masked nature/gesture imagery at 18–26s, increasingly expressive color and processed portraits at 30–42s, softer organic imagery at 46–50s, and sparse black closing frames at 54–60s. BUCK’s case study confirms its combination of precise vectors, organic textures, processed footage, and reusable motion assets.

**Mechanism:** A consistent vocabulary of masks, grids, dots, and contours allows variation without visual incoherence. Increasing color range and density expresses creative possibility; the final reduction in complexity restores attention to the message.

**Proposed recipe — Living System, 18s:** 0–4s establish a controlled grid; 4–8s introduce imagery through repeated masks; 8–13s expand color and spatial freedom; 13–15s combine the system into one hero composition; 15–18s close with quiet typography.

**Agent inputs:** Approved media, focal crops, semantic asset roles, three or four color roles, permitted graphic primitives, a density curve, and closing copy.

**Failure condition:** Random collage has no reading order or relationship to the proposition. Maintain a dominant subject per beat and limit simultaneous motion. Build a reusable scene graph and masking system; changing backgrounds is insufficient. This supports expressive the video editor and the motion engine brand films.

## 6. Figma Config 2026: transform the relationship, not just the object

**Reference:** [Figma: A Symbiosis of Code and Design, Oddfellows](https://oddfellows.tv/work/figma), shown at **Config 2026** before the opening address, according to the studio. No reach metric was established.

**Evidence:** This analysis uses the creator’s production breakdown, not a complete timed inspection. Oddfellows describes separate visual languages for code and design, a nature-based framework, an initial disconnected state, their combination into a shared creative language, and a final vision of collaboration. Exact original timing and audio behavior remain unverified.

**Mechanism:** Two visual systems become interdependent over the narrative. The metaphor explains a relationship: separate capabilities become more useful together. The transformation must be legible at the level of shape, behavior, or material rather than depend entirely on explanatory copy.

**Proposed recipe — Material Language, 18s:** 0–4s introduce two clearly different systems; 4–8s show friction or incomplete interaction; 8–12s establish a shared rule; 12–15s demonstrate a capability requiring both; 15–18s resolve into a unified composition.

**Agent inputs:** The two concepts, their independent visual grammars, a justified relationship, allowed transformations, a common organizing rule, and the final outcome. Original assets may be procedural shapes, typography, or commissioned illustrations.

**Failure condition:** The metaphor implies a technical dependency that is untrue, or the systems merely dissolve into an unrelated logo. Require the agent to explain each visual transition in plain language before rendering. Useful for research-to-editing narratives across the research agent and the video editor, provided the demonstrated relationship is real.

## 7. Google / Ordinary Folk: preserve identity through interface morphs

**Reference:** [Google Montage, Ordinary Folk](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/project/google-montage), **2025**, listed as a **1:12 montage** of work across several Google projects. It is not documented as one continuous product-launch film. No verified reach metric was established.

**Evidence:** Inspection covered the studio’s [15.5s square portfolio excerpt](https://media.ordinary.co/video/home/google-montage.mp4), which is silent. Its offset within the full montage is unknown. Excerpt-local samples show a short central question at 0–1s; an outline path with a star-like tip at 2–3s; a typography beat at 4s; Google identity at 6s; a search/Lens form becoming an AI Mode pill at 7–8s; an input at 9s; arch outlines and voice-related wording at 10s; a frame/photo at 11s; a circular record form at 12–13s; and a capsule/camera push at 14–15s.

**Mechanism:** Persistent geometry lets one recognizable control become the next. Translation, scaling, corner-radius changes, path interpolation, masking, and purposeful holds can create continuity without requiring photorealistic footage.

**Proposed recipe — Elastic Interface, 15s:** 0–3s ask one question; 3–6s transform its container into a tool; 6–10s demonstrate one supported input/action; 10–12s transform into the result; 12–15s hold that result and a concise next step.

**Agent inputs:** Start/end vector shapes, compatible path topology or intermediate shapes, semantic correspondence, real interface states, copy limits, focal anchors, and timing constraints.

**Failure condition:** The morph changes meaning before recognition, distorts text, or combines product capabilities that do not coexist. Separate geometry from live text and insert a hold when semantic identity changes. This is a practical extension of the pack’s deterministic rendering approach.

## Implementation priorities

Build **Obsidian Product Journey** and **Elastic Interface** first: supplied UI states and bounded geometric transitions make their inputs inspectable. Add **Matchline** when asset search and temporal alignment can support it. **Living System** needs stronger composition controls; **Approval Drama** needs suitable performance footage. **Material Language** and **Impossible / Reveal** require deliberate art direction or capture planning before rendering.

Every recipe should declare its story role, required assets, aspect layouts, text limits, evidence requirements, focal regions, readable holds, and fallback. The agent should return an edit plan naming each input, the intended transformation, and the resulting meaning. Keep completed actions distinct from proposed workflows. Asset absence should change the selected recipe rather than silently produce imaginary evidence.

Composition and typography remain decisive: the viewer needs to know where to look, what changed, and why it matters. Motion should answer those questions. A premium finish depends on timing and hierarchy as much as simulation complexity.

## Proposed sound direction

These are original recommendations for the adaptations, not reconstructions of the reference soundtracks. Make each sound serve a narrative event. Matchline can place a transient at the shared contact point. Obsidian Product Journey can use quiet interface cues and a distinct arrival for the useful artifact. Living System can increase texture with visual density, then clear space for the closing line. Approval Drama can reduce the music during the decision hold so a small reply carries weight. Material Language can begin with two distinct sound textures and combine them when the visual systems cooperate. Elastic Interface can pair continuous movement with a restrained settling cue. Impossible Reveal should preserve believable scene ambience until the reveal.

The future agent contract should identify cue role, target frame, source asset, gain, and any permitted timing tolerance. Keep dialogue at its natural speed. The implemented starter renderer accepts one supplied track; it does not yet author this cue mix or align animation to it.

## Test the mechanism with a small controlled comparison

Use the same audience, placement, offer, length, assets, and distribution conditions where possible. Begin with two openings: an immediate result versus an unresolved question. Keep the remainder identical. In a separate comparison, vary only the reveal timing. Avoid changing hook, music, pacing, and proposition simultaneously.

Track the platform’s available early-retention measure, retention around the reveal, completion, and the intended action. Record the denominator and delivery conditions. Small organic comparisons are directional because distribution is uneven; do not call a winner from a handful of views or assume reach equals usefulness. Select the version that improves the intended outcome while preserving comprehension, then repeat with another subject before promoting it to a default.
